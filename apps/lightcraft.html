02/Highlights

Photo library & raw developer

What's inside.

  1. 01

    Non-destructive by design

    A scene-referred, wide-gamut, 32-bit float pipeline. Your originals are never touched.

  2. 02

    Every control you reach for

    Light, color, effects, tone curve, an 8-band color mixer, 3-way color grading wheels and 18 built-in presets.

  3. 03

    Precise masking

    Brush, linear and radial gradients, luminance and color ranges, plus sky, subject and background masks.

  4. 04

    Native raw decoding

    Pure-Rust decoders for DNG, CR2, ARW, NEF, RAF (including X-Trans), RW2, PEF and ORF, with more on the way.

  5. 05

    A library that keeps up

    Albums, ratings, flags, field search and virtualized grids built for big catalogs.

  6. 06

    Instant feedback

    The GPU develop pipeline updates a 24 MP raw in about 4 ms per slider move.
